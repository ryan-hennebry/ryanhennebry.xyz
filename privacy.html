Privacy

Ryan Hennebry

This site keeps a log of each request for 90 days: the time, the page, the referring page, your browser's user agent, and the network and approximate location that Cloudflare attaches to it.

I never store your IP address, only a one-way hash of it and your user agent, made with a random key that changes every day and is deleted after two days. That hash can link your visits within one day and never after.

If a visit looks like a person rather than a bot, I send its address to Cloudflare's DNS and to IPLocate to find the network or company behind it, and email the result to myself.

To have your visits deleted sooner, email me at ryanhennebry@gmail.com with the day and rough time you visited, since nothing I store identifies you directly.
