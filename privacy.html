Privacy

Ryan Hennebry

I send unsolicited reports and job applications, so I log visits here to see which organisations come to look. My lawful basis for this is legitimate interests.

This site keeps a log of each request for 90 days: the time, the page, the referring page, your browser's user agent, and the network and approximate location that Cloudflare attaches to it.

The log does not keep your IP address as such. It keeps a one-way hash of it and your user agent, made with a random key that changes every day and is deleted after two days, so the hash can link your visits within one day and never after.

If a visit looks like a person rather than a bot, I send its address to Cloudflare's DNS and to IPLocate, an Australian company, to find the network or company behind it. The network name that comes back can contain the address itself. I keep what comes back with the visit and email it to myself with the time, page, referring page, network, country, city and browser. Nothing deletes those emails automatically, so they stay in my inbox until I delete them.

To have your visits deleted sooner, email me at ryanhennebry@gmail.com with the day and rough time you visited, so I can find them. You have the right to object to this logging. Email me and I will delete your visits; I cannot recognise you on a later day, so I cannot exclude you in advance. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
